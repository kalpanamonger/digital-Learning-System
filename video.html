Data Representation and Number System

Learn what data representation means and how computers represent information using binary digits. Explore the four main number systems: binary, decimal, octal, and hexadecimal. Understand their bases, place values, bits, and bytes.

Topic: Introduction to Number Systems
LESSON 2

Binary to Other Number Systems

Learn how to convert binary numbers into decimal, octal, and hexadecimal numbers. Understand the place-value method for binary-to-decimal conversion and how to group binary digits when converting to octal or hexadecimal.

Topic: Binary Conversions
LESSON 3

Octal to Other Number Systems

Discover how to convert octal numbers into binary, decimal, and hexadecimal. Practise multiplying each octal digit by its positional power for decimal conversion and learn how binary grouping helps with other conversions.

Topic: Octal Conversions
LESSON 4

Decimal to Other Number Systems

Understand how to convert decimal numbers into binary, octal, and hexadecimal. Learn the repeated-division method, how to record remainders, and why the remainders are read from bottom to top.

Topic: Decimal Conversions
LESSON 5

Hexadecimal to Other Number Systems

Explore the hexadecimal number system, which uses digits 0–9 and letters A–F. Learn how to convert hexadecimal numbers into binary, decimal, and octal, and practise applying the conversion rules step by step.

Topic: Hexadecimal Conversions
